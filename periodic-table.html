Standards this covers

8th Grade — S8P1
Structure of the atom (protons, neutrons, electrons), reading a periodic table square, families and groups, metals vs. nonmetals vs. metalloids, and using position to predict properties.
High School — SPS1, SPS2
Electron arrangement and energy levels, valence electrons, periodic trends in atomic radius, ionization energy and electronegativity, predicting ion charge, and predicting ionic vs. covalent bonding from position.
